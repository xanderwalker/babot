BaBot Motion firmware
====================
Based on the official BaBot firmware at commit:
dca09d19d3fd281651805e8ca3b8ce80e3ee45c1
BaBot: https://ba-bot.com/
Contact: info@ba-bot.com

The BaBot application remains under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial
4.0 International, as supplied in LICENSE. Changes add guarded external USB
motion, OFF-at-boot behavior, smooth servo output and release identification.
